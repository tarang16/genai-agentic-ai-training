id: KB-1004
title: How to update monitor firmware
products: VX2780, VX3218, TD2455, XG2431
---
## Steps
1. Download the vDisplayManager tool from the support site (Windows 10/11 or macOS 13+).
2. Connect the monitor to the PC with the USB upstream cable (USB-B or USB-C), not only the video cable.
3. Open vDisplayManager > Firmware > Check for update. Do not power off the monitor during the update (about 4 minutes).
4. After the update, perform OSD > Setup > All Recall.

## Current firmware
VX2780 1.0.8, TD2455 1.0.8, VX3218 2.1.3, XG2431 1.2.0.

## Notes
A failed update that leaves the power LED blinking amber needs a service-mode recovery: escalate to L2, do not ask the customer to retry repeatedly.
