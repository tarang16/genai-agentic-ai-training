id: KB-1001
title: Monitor shows "No Signal" on HDMI
products: VX2780, VX3218, TD2455
---
## Applies to
All monitors with HDMI input. Most common after a PC/GPU change or a firmware update of the source device.

## Steps
1. Press the Input button and select the HDMI port the cable is actually plugged into (HDMI 1 vs HDMI 2).
2. Reseat both ends of the cable; try a certified High Speed HDMI cable (HDMI 2.0 or later for 4K60).
3. Disable "Auto Input Detect" in OSD > Setup and select the port manually; some GPUs wake too slowly for auto detect.
4. On laptops, press Win+P and choose Duplicate or Extend.
5. Reset the monitor: OSD > Setup > All Recall.

## Resolution rate
Resolves about 85% of HDMI no-signal cases. If none of these work, request a photo of the OSD Information page and escalate to L2.
