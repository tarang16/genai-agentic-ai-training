title: No sound from monitor speakers
products: VX2780, VX3218
---
## Steps
1. On Windows, select the monitor as the output device (Sound settings > Output > the monitor model name).
2. Check OSD > Audio > Audio Input matches the video input (HDMI audio vs Line-in).
3. Make sure Mute is off and Volume is above 0 in OSD; the Windows volume and the monitor volume are separate.
4. DisplayPort to HDMI adapters often drop audio; test with a native HDMI or DisplayPort cable.

## Resolution rate
Resolves about 90% of audio cases. The TD2455 has no speakers; tell the customer politely and suggest the 3.5mm output.
