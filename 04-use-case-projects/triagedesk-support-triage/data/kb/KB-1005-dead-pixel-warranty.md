products: VX2780, VX3218, TD2455, XG2431

## Policy
- Standard warranty is 3 years from the invoice date, panel and backlight included.
- Zero Bright Dot policy: a single always-on (bright) sub-pixel within the first 30 days qualifies for replacement.
- Dark (dead) pixels: replacement if 5 or more dark pixels, or 2 or more within the central zone.

## What to collect
1. Invoice copy with date of purchase.
2. Serial number from the label on the back or OSD > Information.
3. A photo of the screen showing the pixel on a solid white and a solid black background.

## Process
Create an RMA in Freshdesk using the "RMA - Panel" template. Advance replacement is available only to business customers on the Premium plan.
