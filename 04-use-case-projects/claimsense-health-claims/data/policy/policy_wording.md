# CarePlus Individual Health Insurance: Policy Wording (training sample)

This is a fictional policy wording written for a training exercise. Structure and terminology follow common
Indian retail health insurance products, but the numbers are illustrative. Do not use for real claims.

## 2.1 Sum Insured
The Sum Insured (SI) is the maximum the Company will pay in a policy year for all claims combined. Claims are
payable up to the balance Sum Insured available on the date of admission.

## 2.2 In-patient Hospitalisation
The Company will pay Medical Expenses for In-patient Care when the Insured Person is hospitalised for at least
24 consecutive hours on the advice of a Medical Practitioner, for an Illness or Injury contracted or sustained
during the Policy Period. The 24-hour limit does not apply to Day Care Treatment (clause 2.4).

## 2.3 Pre- and Post-hospitalisation
Medical Expenses incurred up to 30 days before admission and up to 60 days after discharge, for the same
condition, are payable provided the In-patient claim is admissible.

## 2.4 Day Care Treatment
Procedures listed in Annexure II (including cataract surgery, lithotripsy, chemotherapy, dialysis and
tonsillectomy) are covered even if hospitalisation is less than 24 hours.

## 3.1 Initial Waiting Period
No claim is payable for any Illness diagnosed or treatment taken within the first 30 days from the first
Policy inception date. This waiting period does not apply to hospitalisation due to an Accident.

## 3.2 Specific Disease Waiting Period
The following conditions and their surgeries are covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage from the
first Policy inception date: cataract; benign prostatic hypertrophy; hernia of all types; hydrocele;
fistula and fissure in ano; haemorrhoids; sinusitis; tonsillitis; gall bladder stones (cholelithiasis);
kidney and urinary stones; joint replacement surgery unless necessitated by Accident; varicose veins.

## 3.3 Pre-existing Disease Waiting Period
Expenses related to any Pre-existing Disease (PED) declared in the proposal form, and its direct
complications, are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage from the first Policy inception date.

## 4.1 Room Rent Limit and Proportionate Deduction
Room rent (including boarding and nursing) is payable up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day for a normal room and
2% of the Sum Insured per day for ICU. If the Insured Person occupies a room with a higher rent than eligible,
all Associated Medical Expenses are paid in the same proportion as the eligible room rent bears to the actual
room rent. Associated Medical Expenses means room rent, nursing charges, surgeon and consultant fees and
operation theatre charges. Proportionate deduction does NOT apply to pharmacy and medicines, consumables,
implants and diagnostic investigations.

## 4.2 Co-payment
A co-payment of 20% applies to every admissible claim if the Insured Person was aged 61 years or more at the
time of first Policy inception. Co-payment is applied after all other deductions.

## 4.3 Cataract Sub-limit
Cataract treatment is payable up to INR 40,000 per eye per policy year.

## 5.1 Exclusion: Cosmetic and Plastic Surgery
Treatment to change appearance is excluded unless needed for reconstruction following an Accident, burn or
cancer, or as part of medically necessary treatment certified by the attending Medical Practitioner.

## 5.2 Exclusion: Dental Treatment
Dental treatment is excluded unless it requires hospitalisation following an Accident.

## 5.3 Exclusion: Alcohol and Substance Abuse
Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse, or any addictive condition, and their consequences, is
excluded. A diagnosis in which alcohol use is documented as a contributing cause falls under this exclusion.

## 5.4 Exclusion: Obesity and Weight Control
Surgery for obesity is excluded unless BMI is 40 or more, or 35 or more with severe co-morbidities, and
conservative treatment has failed, as certified by the Medical Practitioner.

## 5.5 Exclusion: Non-medical Items
Items listed in Annexure I (non-medical expenses), such as toiletries, attendant food, gloves supplied for
personal use, registration and admission charges, and documentation charges, are not payable.

## 6.1 Claim Intimation and Documents
Planned hospitalisation must be intimated at least 48 hours before admission; emergency hospitalisation within
24 hours of admission. Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of discharge with: claim form,
discharge summary, final bill with breakup, payment receipts, investigation reports, and for Accidents a
medico-legal certificate or FIR where applicable.

## 6.2 Claim Decision
Every repudiation (rejection) must be communicated in writing citing the specific policy clause relied upon.
The Company will settle or reject a claim within 30 days of receiving the last necessary document.

## Annexure I: Non-medical items (extract)
Admission kit, registration charges, attendant food and beverages, toiletries, baby food, cosmetics,
telephone charges, laundry, documentation and medical records charges, ambulance collar or cervical
collar purchased for home use, gloves and masks charged in excess of procedure requirements.
